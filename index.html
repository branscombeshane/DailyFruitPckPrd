      Import the Sage product list

      Export Sage's item/product list (code, description, category) as a CSV. New products get added there periodically, so re-export and re-import this every so often (e.g. daily) to pick up anything new — importing only ever adds products that aren't in the list below yet; it never changes the category of a product that's already there, so anything you've classified here won't get overwritten by a stale or re-categorised Sage export.
